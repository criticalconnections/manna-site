Privacy policy

Effective September 21, 2026. Manna is made by YOUR-NAME-OR-COMPANY.

What stays on your device

Your reading preferences, saved verses, highlights, notebooks and handwritten pages, study progress, scenery choices and reminder times are stored on your device. They leave it only when you export or share them yourself. Deleting the app deletes your library from that device.

What Manna does not collect

There are no Manna accounts. Manna does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or reading history, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Subscriptions

Manna Plus is sold by Apple through the App Store; Apple handles payment and your Apple Account. To confirm your access, Manna uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and the record of your purchase: the product, its dates and its status. It never receives your name, Apple ID or payment details. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications; no notification service is involved. Widgets read the day's verse from the app's own storage on your device.

Children

Manna does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
