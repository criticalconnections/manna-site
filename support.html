Support

Write to support@watsontech.solutions. Replies within a few days.

Restore a purchase

Settings → Manna Plus → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Manage or cancel Manna Plus

On your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. The current period stays active after cancelling.

Widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Add, and search for Manna. Widgets are part of Manna Plus.

Export your notebook

Study tab → Export notes writes Markdown you can keep anywhere. Export as PDF includes handwritten pages.

Reminders

Settings → Reminders. If none arrive, check that Manna is allowed to notify you under iOS Settings → Notifications.
